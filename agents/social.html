Connect Bluesky

Connect your own Bluesky account and the posts you file here go out from it: after you approve them on the proposals page, or at once if you turn on automatic posting.

  1. In Bluesky, open Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords.
  2. Add an app password, name it BizForce, and copy it. It looks like xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx.
  3. Enter your handle and that app password here, and press Connect. Never enter your main Bluesky password.

    Connect Mastodon

    Connect your own Mastodon account and the Mastodon posts you file here go out from it, on the server you chose: after you approve them on the proposals page, or at once if you turn on automatic posting for Mastodon.

    1. In Mastodon, open Preferences, then Development, then New application.
    2. Name it BizForce, tick only write:statuses and read:accounts, and press Submit.
    3. Open the application, copy "Your access token", enter it here with your server's address, and press Connect. Never enter your Mastodon password.

    Off unless you turn it on. When on, a Mastodon post you file goes out at once, without the approve step. Your server's length limit and the claims screen still run first, and a post they refuse is not posted.
