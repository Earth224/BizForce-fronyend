2. How we use it

  • To run the service you ask for: your agents, storefront, listings, wallet and trades, messages, outreach and documents.
  • When you rate an agent's result, pin one as an example or give an agent a preference, BizForce stores it and uses it only in that agent's prompts for you, below every rule, so the agent writes the way you asked; the AI model itself does not change. Your ratings, examples and preferences are private to you, are in your data download, and are deleted with your account.
  • To bill your subscription, through Stripe.
  • To email you about your account: confirming your address, resetting your password, and similar messages you need. Marketing email goes only to people who confirmed they want it, and every one has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep BizForce safe: rate limits, spotting abuse, and enforcing the Terms.
  • To keep the records money and trades need, such as the wallet ledger other members' records depend on.

3. Who it goes to

We do not share your personal information for advertising.

We do not sell your data. BizForce's server contains no advertising or third-party analytics code, and no page loads a tracker. Your data is sent only where a feature you use needs it: Supabase (the database), Railway (the server), Netlify (the pages), Anthropic (the AI agents), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Twilio (text messages), Bluesky and Mastodon (replies sent through outreach), and your browser's push service (notifications you turned on). A web page an agent is asked to read receives only the request for that page. Some pages also have your browser fetch files from Google Fonts (typefaces), jsDelivr (chart, QR-code and image libraries), Cloudflare's cdnjs (the PDF library on the digital cards page), and YouTube and Vimeo (a video a member adds to their storefront); your browser contacts these directly, so they see your IP address, your browser and the page's address, but none of your account data. A picture, recording or video another member links on their storefront or listing loads from wherever that member put it.

4. How it is protected

Exactly as the Your data page states it:

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Stored with our database host, Supabase. How the stored database is encrypted on disk is the host's setting, not something BizForce's code does or can show, so this page claims nothing about it.
  • Your password is stored only as a bcrypt hash, never as the password itself.
  • AI provider API keys you add are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) before they are stored.
  • Backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key held by the owner, and without that key no backup is made. Sessions, passkey challenges, API keys and push credentials are never put in a backup.
  • Not end-to-end encrypted: BizForce's servers can read your data so the agents can work with it.

5. On your device

  • BizForce keeps your sign-in in your browser's own storage, and sets no cookies of its own. Signing out clears every BizForce key that belongs to you from this browser.
  • Some preferences stay on the device after sign-out, such as the editor's colours and where panels sit; they hold nothing about your account.
  • When you ask Termaximus's Guide, it sends your question with only the page you are on, which panels and tab are open, whether an empty list or an error banner is showing, and the last 5 pages you visited; nothing typed into the page is sent, and that list of pages stays only in this browser tab.
  • "Keep a copy on this device for offline use" is off unless you turn it on: on this page when you are signed in, or in Settings, under Account.
  • When it is on, this browser keeps a copy of what you last loaded of your recent tasks and their results; your business profile; your agents and their last runs; your wallet balance and history; your account details (email address, profile, and whether your plan is active); the description on the Your data page, each with the time it was saved.
  • The copy is kept in this browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on this device, up to about 5 MB, and is not sent anywhere. BizForce's servers do not hold it.
  • It never holds your sign-in tokens, payment details, the download of your data, or other members' private data.
  • Turning the setting off deletes the copy at once. Signing out deletes it and turns the setting off.
  • Offline, a page with a saved copy shows it with the time it was saved, and a page without one says it needs a connection. Nothing can be sent or changed until you're back online, and nothing is kept to send later.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • We keep your data while your account exists, so the service can work.
  • You can download all of it, or delete your account, at any time on the Your data page.
  • Deleting needs your username, typed exactly, and your password. Every account has a password; if you have forgotten it, reset it from the sign-in page first.
  • It is refused while a paid subscription is active, in a trial or past due. Cancel it first on Billing; then delete.
  • It cannot be undone. Download your data first if you want a copy.
  • Every sign-in is ended and the account can never sign in again.
  • Your email address can be used to open a new account afterwards. The new account starts empty: nothing from this one carries over.
  • Your username is released, and another member may take it.
  • Your files are deleted (pictures, audio, video, book and document files you uploaded), except files you sold, which stay available to the members who bought them. If any could not be removed right away, they are removed automatically within a few days.

Some records are kept after an account is deleted, because other members' records depend on them or the law expects them:

  • Your account: Emptied: your email, password, business name, industry, bio, picture, and sign-in tokens and addresses are removed. A record with only its id, dates, role and billing status stays, because the orders, ledgers and messages other members hold point at it. It can never sign in again.
  • Every email sent to your contacts, including your own account email, with its status: A send that bounced or was marked as spam is kept unchanged, address included, because it stops every member's mail to that address, which protects the person it was sent to. Other sends are kept, with the address hashed, only to contacts whose consent record is kept; the rest are deleted.
  • When each of your contacts agreed to, confirmed or withdrew consent, and where: Kept unchanged, because it is the proof that messages already sent through BizForce had consent, needed to answer a complaint or claim about them. The contact it belongs to is kept with its email and phone hashed and its name removed.
  • Your contacts: Kept as consent evidence, with email addresses and phone numbers replaced by a one-way hash and names removed: a contact with a consent record is kept, hashed, so that record still has someone to belong to; contacts without one are deleted.
  • People who gave you their email or phone, and what they consented to: Kept as consent evidence, with email addresses and phone numbers replaced by a one-way hash and names removed: a capture that recorded consent is the proof of it; captures without consent are deleted.
  • Your SMS subscribers and their consent: Kept as consent evidence, with email addresses and phone numbers replaced by a one-way hash and names removed: it is the proof that texts already sent had consent.
  • Every text sent for you: Kept, with each phone number replaced by a one-way hash. The words of each text stay, because this is the record of what was sent, needed to answer a complaint about a text.
  • Signatures you made, and signatures on your documents: Deleted on your own documents. Kept on other members' documents, because a signature you made on another member's document is their signed record.
  • What you listed for sale: Deleted, with their files, except a listing another member bought: it is taken off sale and kept, with its file and pictures, because the member who bought it downloads their file through it.
  • Your BizCash balance: Your balance is forfeited: BizCash has no cash value; the balance is forfeited in a final ledger row, so the balance still equals the sum of its ledger.
  • Every BizCash movement in and out of your wallet: Kept, because it is one half of a money record another member also holds, and a ledger with a row missing no longer adds up.
  • What you bought and sold on the marketplace: Kept, because the other member's purchase or sale is the same order.
  • Donations you made, and donations to your campaigns: Kept, because the other member's donation record is the same row.
  • BizCash you sent to other members and received from them, as a transfer or as support: Kept, because the other member's transfer is the same record.
  • Your subscription: Kept, because it is the billing record that matches the payment provider's, kept for accounts and for answering a refund or dispute.
  • Payments recorded from your subscription: Kept, because it is the money ledger kept for the accounts.
  • What each AI model call cost: Kept, because it is BizForce's record of what it paid for.
  • YouTube API quota recorded against your account, if any: Kept, because it is the daily quota ledger.
  • Messages you sent to and received from other members: Kept, because the other member's conversation is the same messages.
  • Moderation actions about your account, or taken by you as an admin: Kept, because it is the only record of who was banned and why.
  • Flags an admin put on your account: Kept, because it is the only record of an admin's flag.

On the records that show who took part, you are shown as a deleted member.

Encrypted nightly backups made before you delete your account still contain your data until they are deleted; they can be read only with the owner's key. YouTube data is deleted after 30 days (section 9).

7. Your privacy rights, including in California

Whoever you are, you can:

  • Know what we hold: the Your data page lists it, and its download gives you all of it.
  • Delete it: delete your account on the Your data page.
  • Correct it: edit your profile and settings, or write to us.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. None occurs: we do not sell or share it.
  • Use these rights without being treated differently for it.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request we cannot handle through the Your data page, email support@bizforceai.net from the address on your account, with "Privacy request" in the subject. We may ask you to confirm it is you, and we answer within 45 days. An authorised agent can make a request for you with your signed permission.

8. Children

BizForce is not for anyone under 18, and nobody under 18 may create an account. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete its data.

9. YouTube

BizForce uses YouTube API Services. By using the YouTube section of Lead Radar you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and YouTube data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What is accessed: public YouTube videos found by searching a fixed list of questions BizForce chooses, and the public comments under them: the comment text, the commenter's public display name, the video's link and the comment's time. No member's data is sent to YouTube, and BizForce does not sign in to anyone's YouTube or Google account.
  • What is stored: those public comments, as leads in Lead Radar's YouTube section.
  • How long: at most 30 days. Older YouTube comments are deleted automatically every day.
  • Not combined: YouTube comments are shown only in their own section. They are not scored, not replied to through outreach, not counted in demand figures, and not combined with data from any other source.
