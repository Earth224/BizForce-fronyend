Refunds

Last updated October 30, 2026 · Version 2026-10-30

The short version: cancel any time, keep what you paid for, and no partial refunds.

The policy

  • You can cancel any time.
  • Your access continues to the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • There are no partial or prorated refunds.

An All Access subscription is $199 per month and renews automatically until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not end the month you are in, so you keep access to its end and are not charged again.

How to cancel

  1. Sign in and open Billing.
  2. Choose "Manage / Cancel Subscription". This opens Stripe's secure billing page for your account.
  3. Choose to cancel, and confirm.

Billing then shows that your subscription will not renew, with the date your access ends.

If something looks wrong

If you think you were charged in error, write to support@bizforceai.net with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. If the law where you live gives you a right to a refund, this policy does not take it away.
