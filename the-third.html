The Inner I.Q Test

A cognition diagnostic, remastered — mapping the faculties of a mind without ranking the worth of a person.

The intelligence test you were raised to trust was not built to help you. Alfred Binet designed the first scale in 1905 to find French schoolchildren who needed more support — a humane aim. American eugenicists took his work and inverted it: into a ruler for innate human worth, used to screen immigrants at Ellis Island, to justify forced sterilization, and to manufacture the score gaps later used to segregate and diminish Black Americans. The number it produced was never neutral. The Inner I.Q Test keeps the real diagnostic craft — the measuring of genuine cognitive faculties — and severs it from the ranking. It returns not a verdict but a constellation. Six faculties, lit to the degree you carry them, including one the old instrument could not even conceive of. Know thyself.
